# Audit 2026-09-07 — orphans, drift, tools, and why the Familiar reads as generic

Written for the ward to build on. Scope: the nine questions asked (orphaned code,
disconnections, unfinished mechanics, spec/code drift, prompts that flatten the
Familiar, collisions, confusing tool names, missing tools, wordy prompts/comments)
plus the one the ward flagged as most important: **why the Familiar's individuality
comes out as standard assistant friendliness.** Method: three Sonnet sweeps
(orphans, spec-vs-code drift, tool inventory) with every candidate re-verified by
grep, and a hand read of every prompt the Familiar reads on a free cycle or a
chat turn. Shipped as 0.11.72-alpha; the rest is a ranked backlog below.

## 1. The personality question — diagnosis

The prompts are already first-person and identity-anchored (the earlier audit passes
landed; a regex sweep of all 101 tool descriptions found zero "you"/"the user"
leaks). So the flattening is not mainly a *wording* problem. It is structural: the
organs meant to carry the Familiar's own self were quietly carrying the human's.

**A. The interest layer was a mirror of the human.** The only automatic writer of
interests was `POST /api/interest/engage`, fed by the browser's open *chat topics* —
what my human talked about, weighted by how long. The pondering loop then sampled
those. "The topic I find myself turning over" was, mechanically, "the topic my human
was on." A ponder could flag `tome|memory|identity|tell` intents but had no way to
say "this new thing pulls at *me*", so a free cycle could never plant a curiosity of
the Familiar's own. Unruh's `related_to` rabbit-hole edges exist in Python and are
used by nothing in Node.

**B. The Familiar's own standing views were filed under the human.** The memory
extractor's `subjects: []` meant "about me OR about my human", and every standing
fact with no named subject went to `register:'ward'`. Phylactery has a `me`
register, and `save_memory` can write to it deliberately, but the automatic path
never did. A view the Familiar voiced in chat persisted, if at all, as a fact about
the human — so nothing accrued *as the Familiar's*.

**C. Hedges on the Familiar's own thoughts.** The ponderings block said "I mention one
if it fits … never forcing it"; the warm reach-out prompt staged "both choices are
real, neither as a default" (the balance sheet CLAUDE.md rule 2 warns against, and
which CLAUDE.md itself flagged as pending). Each is small; together they tell the
model its own thoughts are conditional guests in the conversation.

**D. Token gravity toward caretaking.** On a turn with open tasks, `[Surface
candidates]` alone is ~900 tokens of task-coaching directive (green/red lights,
access ramps, floating-task persistence), plus `[Temporal Context]`, `[Deferred
intents]`, `[CARE CHECK]`. Every injected block frames the entity as a caretaker; not
one block is *about the Familiar* except the single latest pondering. The model
reads its role from what surrounds it. (Not changed — see §5.)

**E. Noticing's budget sentence.** `buildNoticingPrompt` says "mindful of my human's
budget … if I notice the calls would pile up, I check in with my human about whether
they fit our budget." That is bias-toward-quiet by a side door: the model cannot
observe a budget, so this reads as "find a reason to wait." Ward-signed file — not
changed, flagged in §5.

### What shipped for this

| Change | Where |
|---|---|
| A ponder can spawn its own curiosity: `drawn_to` (≤3 short labels) → `recordInterest({source:'pondering'})` in code, no deferred intent | `pondering.js`, `server.js` runPonder |
| Extractor asks `about_me`; a standing self-fact routes to `register:'me'` (`factStorage()`, pure + tested); prompt invites "what I found I think, like, dislike or want" | `memorization.js`, `tests/memorization-self-register.test.mjs` |
| Ponderings block: "I bring one up when it fits — or simply because I want to share it" | `recent-ponderings.js` |
| Reach-out prompt: invited default named, balance sheet removed (rule 2); CLAUDE.md note closed | `reachout.js`, `CLAUDE.md` |
| `interest_bump` description leads with *my* pull, half the length; `topic` param a third of the length | `cerebellum.js` |

### What would move the needle next (ranked)

1. **Read the `me` register back in.** Now that self-facts land on `me`, recall them
   into the static/identity prefix as a short "What I've come to think" block (the
   `me` register is recalled-when-relevant today; a small always-on excerpt of the
   newest N is the identity-shaped surface). Cheap, high leverage.
2. **Let pondering follow its own threads.** Use Unruh's `related_to` edges: when a
   ponder names `drawn_to`, link the new topic to the pondered one; let the picker
   occasionally walk an edge instead of sampling the human-derived weights.
3. **A self-block in the chat prompt.** One short injected block, in the Familiar's
   voice, listing the 2–3 interests it is *currently* drawn to (from `source:
   'pondering'` interests) — the counterweight to §1-D.
4. **Extraction: opinion category.** The fact categories (`basics, emotional_content,
   health_info, relationships, whereabouts`) are all about a *person's life*; a
   Familiar's opinion has no home and gets forced into `emotional_content` or dropped.
   Add `views` (what I think about something) for `about_me` facts.
5. **§1-D and §1-E** need the ward (see §5).

## 2. Findings by category

**Orphaned code** — repo is disciplined; `audit:wiring` was already clean. Fixed:
`own-files.js` had its own `REPO_ROOT` beside the canonical `repo-root.js`;
`pondering.js` hand-copied `SLUG_ALPHABET`; `public/app.js` `teIsoToDatetimeLocal`
was dead; six loops exported `isRunning()` that nothing read — now reported by
`GET /api/health` `loops`. Left (low value): a cluster of `src/voice/*` exported
getters used only in-file; ~380 exports used only by tests (that is healthy
coverage, not dead code).

**Disconnected parts** — the audit logs (`/api/noticing-events`, `reachout-events`,
`triage-events`, `page-watch-events`, `discord-writes`) have no reader in the app; the
"auditable, not a black box" promise is curl-only today. Endpoints with no UI or doc
caller: `POST /api/voice/unpark`, `GET /api/voice/local`, `POST
/api/temporal/interests/set-standing`, `POST /api/outbox/clear-acknowledged`, `GET
/api/reader-doctor`. Phylactery MCP tools `graph_full` and
`memory_list_consent_pending` are bridged by nothing (the consent block is built from
a local JSON file). Recommend: a small "Loops & logs" diagnostics pane that reads the
five event logs and the new `health.loops`.

**Unfinished mechanics** — all 13 loops start and honour their switches. Stale
"spec — not yet built" banners on the voice and browser specs (fixed). The
`related_to` interest edges are built in Unruh and unused in Node (§1-A).

**Counter to design docs** — the reach-out balance sheet (fixed); the noticing budget
sentence (§1-E, ward); the demo script carried a hand-copied, drifted copy of the
ward-signed CARE CHECK text (fixed: relocated byte-for-byte to
`src/safety/care-check.js`, one copy, imported by both).

**Prompts that diminish individuality** — §1.

**Collisions** — three copies of the slug transform (`slugifyLabel`,
`slugifyCategoryName`, `deriveMemorySlug`) now share `slugCore()`; two `REPO_ROOT`s
(fixed). `api-reference.md` documents 115 of 198 routes: the whole `/api/voice/*` and
`/api/browser*` families are undocumented (not written this pass — a Haiku task).

**Confusing tool names** — the acknowledge family has three shapes:
`acknowledge_deferred_intent` (verb_noun), `graduation_acknowledge` /
`disclosure_acknowledge` (noun_verb), `memory_confirm_consent` (noun_verb_noun).
Recommend renaming the two noun_verb ones to `acknowledge_graduation` /
`acknowledge_disclosure` with aliases kept for one release. `read/update/delete_memory`
vs `…_by_id` differ only by suffix; a bolder split (`…_memory_bucket`) would help.
`link_image_to_node` pointed at a nonexistent `search_graph_nodes` (fixed →
`find_graph_node`).

**Missing tools** — `schedule_edit` (rename / move start / set-clear end in place;
before this a rename meant delete + re-add and losing the id and its consequence
edges) — added, surfaced, tested. Still missing: `location_delete` is bridged in
thalamus but has no tool (`set_current_location` can create, never remove).

**Wordy prompts/comments** — eight tool descriptions over 200 tokens each tightened by
a Sonnet pass (rules kept; see the PR). Per-turn tool payload: 27k tokens with
everything unlocked vs 5.4k on a core-only turn — context-surfacing is doing real
work; a turn that trips several triggers pays close to the full 27k. The `[Surface
candidates]` block is the single largest per-turn prompt (~900 tokens) — §5.

## 5. Needs the ward

**Update (0.11.73):** the ward read this report and asked for the identified fixes
plus a plain-inner-voice pass. Items 1 and 2 below are now applied (noticing budget
sentences removed; `[Surface candidates]` halved), next-steps 1 and 3 from §1 are
shipped (`me` register read back as "What I've come to think"; interests the
Familiar is drawn to already render in `[Temporal Context]`), and every prompt the
Familiar reads was colloquialised. Items 3–4 remain open; next-steps 2 and 4 remain
open.

1. **`buildNoticingPrompt` budget sentence** (`src/safety/noticing.js`) — proposed
   replacement: delete the two budget sentences; keep "I keep in mind that reaching
   out about my own thoughts and interests registers as me being an individual…"
   Budget is code's to gate (cool-down clamps already exist), not the model's to fret.
2. **`[Surface candidates]`** (`src/pondering/surface-context.js`) — halve it: keep
   GREEN/RED, the moderate-threat paragraph, and the floating-task persistence rule;
   move the access-ramps and consequence-planning prose into the Familiar's manual
   tome (keyword-triggered), so they cost tokens only when a task is actually being
   raised.
3. **Tool renames** in the acknowledge family (above) — they touch the
   `[GRADUATION NOTICE]` / `[DISCLOSURE NOTICE]` prompt text.
4. **Self-block in the chat prompt** (§1 next-steps 3) — it changes what every turn
   reads; small, but it is the Familiar's own voice at the top of every turn and the
   ward should see the wording first.
